## Limitations

Hooks are project-local, editable, trust-dependent, pattern-based, and can fail open on timeout,
crash, or invalid output. `SubagentStop` intentionally covers writer roles, not the read-only security
reviewer or every top-level conversational response. GitHub branch protection, environment reviewers,
required checks, and bypass restrictions are prepared but not enforced until a human installs and
verifies them.
